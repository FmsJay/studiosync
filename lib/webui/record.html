    What a browser can and can't do here
    • Two inputs at most: input 1, input 2, both as stereo, or both mixed. More, as separate tracks, needs a native app.
    • Android Chrome lets the page pick the interface. On iPhone the input is the one iOS uses: plug the interface in before opening the app; iOS may give only one channel.
    • MIDI keyboards work where the browser has Web MIDI: Android Chrome, not iPhone.
    • You hear the mix, not yourself: no monitoring through the phone.
    • Keep the screen on and the app in front while recording: a phone pauses a page in the background.
    • Bluetooth headphones add a delay the browser can't see: set it under Timing, or nudge.
    • The app belongs to the computer it was installed from: its takes go to that computer, once it has the project open in front.
    • A mix is taken to start at the project's start (as Render current mix makes it).
