# Review of 2026-11-16 (0.58.86): what's still open

Four reviewers read StudioSync 0.58.86 for problems like the take-comp bug: state treated as local that changes the sound,
automatic edits that can lose work, robustness, and what collaborators and guests can reach. 0.58.87 fixed the ones listed in
docs/TEST-PLAN.md under 0.58.87. These are still open, roughly most important first.

## What mirror sync loses or wrongly shares
- Automation items on **send** envelopes never travel (mirror.lua: POOLEDENVINST dropped, send envelopes left out of the automation-item lines).
- Automation items on **plug-in parameter** envelopes carry the sender's pool id; the pool itself never travels.
- Looped, rate-changed or trimmed automation items rebuild differently (D_POOL_QNLEN not sent; points read without the one-loop flag).
- Plug-ins with a modulated knob, or on the restless list, leave their saved data out of the hash: a sampler patch or an IR loaded
  with no exposed knob change never goes out; inside a container, one modulated plug-in hides every plug-in's data in it.
- Chain changes made with no plug-in window open in the last minute (a script, the reaper-daemon's set_fx_preset, an action) read as
  reload noise and aren't sent.
- Track group numbers travel raw: two people making "group 5" apart tie each other's tracks together (item groups were made local for this).
- MIDI items with two or more takes never fall back to the print, and the print is always of the first MIDI take.
- Project-wide defaults never travel after sharing: PANLAW, PANMODE, PANLAWFLAGS, ITEMMIX, DEFPITCHMODE, PLAYRATE.
- The master's FX button isn't shared.
- Wrongly shared: take-envelope view lines inside items; possibly lane-size view bits in C_LANESETTINGS; possibly WAK.

## Automatic edits
- Markers and regions are deleted and re-added: their numbers change (and possibly render-matrix assignments).
- Display-only changes reach the saved file: person colours on tracks, and track FX switched off while a print plays.
- A put-back of your edit to someone else's existing item keeps no copy (new items go to a "(yours, not sent)" track; edits don't).
- Incoming updates dissolve local item grouping and MIDI pools (by design, but surprising).
- A "together" start from someone else stops your transport and switches your tab.
- Ctrl+Z on "takes became lanes" is redone by the next pass.

## Robustness
- A shared-folder update can load half-new, half-old files (only band.lua's VERSION is checked): a files.txt with sizes would fix it.
- Settings kept only in persisted ExtState are lost on a crash (studios list, security pins, machine id): mirror them to a file.
- Catching up reads every new event file in one go (only applying is time-limited); the panel's "while you were away" decodes every event.
- Clock differences between computers are never detected (presence goes by the sender's clock).
- Live sync lists the events folder many times a second per tab (quick_transport and others).
- A refused change of an older kind over 10 minutes old is dropped for good if it arrived before the handover it depends on.
- trim.lua's cross-drive archive fallback never removes the original; a root at a drive's top archives inside the shared folder.
- `require "imgui" "0.10"` fails with REAPER's box on an older ReaImGui.

## Access
- A collaborator who is a full member of the studio's Tailscale network, or tagged tag:bandsync, reaches every device and port on
  it: StudioSync's policy rewrite keeps autogroup:member and tag:bandsync reaching everything. Needs a decision: limit tag:bandsync
  to StudioSync's ports (22000, 2049, 10999), and invite collaborators as tagged devices rather than members.
- REAPER's web interface on port 8080 has no password (the owner's own setting, not StudioSync's).
- A member can bring in new computers: they share the folder from their own Syncthing and every computer adds the newcomer.
- Unindexed files are believed (trust.lua treats "" from Syncthing as trusted): a differently cased folder name might pass.
