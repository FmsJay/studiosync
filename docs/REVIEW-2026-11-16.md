# Review of 2026-11-16 (0.58.86): what's still open

Four reviewers read StudioSync 0.58.86 for problems like the take-comp bug: state treated as local that changes the sound,
automatic edits that can lose work, robustness, and what collaborators and guests can reach. 0.58.87 fixed the ones listed in
docs/TEST-PLAN.md under 0.58.87. These are still open, roughly most important first.
0.58.91 fixed the sync-behaviour and robustness items below except where noted. 0.58.89 fixed: automation items on sends, plug-in envelopes and loops; plug-in data inside containers; preset loads by scripts; project
defaults; half-arrived updates; the crash-safe settings file; and Health now warns about clocks and track group clashes (warned, not
renumbered). Left, with why:
- Display-only changes reach the saved file: person colours on tracks, and track FX switched off while a print plays. Each track keeps the
  band's value (P_EXT band_colour / band_fxen) and StudioSync puts it back on every open; only opening the .rpp without StudioSync shows
  them. REAPER gives scripts no hook before a save, and a menu-extension hook would leave the project unsaved right after every Ctrl+S.

## What mirror sync loses or wrongly shares
- MIDI items with two or more takes never fall back to the print, and the print is always of the first MIDI take.
- Left on purpose: C_LANESETTINGS and WAK still travel. Making either local changes every track's or chain's fingerprint, so every
  computer would send everything once and re-render every print, for a view setting nobody notices.

## Automatic edits
- Display-only changes reach the saved file: person colours on tracks, and track FX switched off while a print plays.
- Left on purpose: incoming updates still dissolve local MIDI pools (item groups are kept since 0.58.91). Kept, one bandmate's change
  would spread to the other pooled copies here and go out as your change.

## Robustness
- (all fixed in 0.58.91)

## Access
- A collaborator who is a full member of the studio's Tailscale network, or tagged tag:bandsync, reaches every device and port on
  it: StudioSync's policy rewrite keeps autogroup:member and tag:bandsync reaching everything. Needs a decision: limit tag:bandsync
  to StudioSync's ports (22000, 2049, 10999), and invite collaborators as tagged devices rather than members.
- REAPER's web interface on port 8080 has no password (the owner's own setting, not StudioSync's).
- A member can bring in new computers: they share the folder from their own Syncthing and every computer adds the newcomer.
- Unindexed files are believed (trust.lua treats "" from Syncthing as trusted): a differently cased folder name might pass.
